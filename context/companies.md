## Company 1: PROAGENT, Realtor.

**What it is:** Real estate Agent - I help sellers sell homes for the most amount of money in the timeline that they want it and buyers to purchase properties and get a really good deal.

**Your role:** I'm the owner.

**Revenue model:** This business makes money on a commission of every transaction closed.

**Products / Services:**

| Product/Service | Price | Audience | Description |
|----------------|-------|----------|-------------|
| Home Buying/Selling Services | 3% commission | Sellers and buyers in Houston area | Help sellers sell homes for max money in desired timeline, buyers get good deals |

**Target Clients:**

**Ideal Client:** Above $350k seller, motivated to sell, looking to buy and sell

**Other Niches:**
- Distressed sellers (homes going through foreclosure, in forbearance)
- Absentee owners
- Out of state owners
- Move-up owners
- Retired owners
- Downsize owners

**Sales Process - Cold Calling:**

**Goal:** Get 2 appointments per day, with 3 appointments set to 1 actually attended, and 2 attended appointments to get 1 listing

**Contact Ratio:** Takes about 24-30 contacts to get 1 appointment

**Duplicability:** This process should be duplicable for any ISA or agent added to the team in the future

**Daily Schedule - Business Generation:**

**8:00 AM - 11:00 AM:** Cold calling for sales (sellers and/or buyers)

**9:30 AM - 10:00 AM:** Breakfast Club — daily sales training (Chad Cooley, COLLAB)

**11:30 AM - 12:00 PM or 1:00 PM:** Calling agents (recruitment)

**End of Day:**
- Download today's Breakfast Club recording → drop in `~/breakfast-club/inbox/` → auto-processes in background (~30 seconds of effort)
- Run `/sync` in Claude to sync all data sources (GHL, ads, email)

**Current state:** Its in its growth phase, right now closing about 1.5 deals a month and need to be growing and scaling to at least 2-3 a month by the end of the year

## Company 2: COLLAB

**What it is:** Recruiting real estate agents into my brokerage where they get resources to do more transactions or scale up in the way they want

**Your role:** Right now, it's mostly sales and marketing to recruit.

**Revenue model:** It makes money off of every closed transaction of each agent that produces and closes on a transaction. Caps out when they are capped.

**Products / Services:**

| Product/Service | Price | Audience | Description |
|----------------|-------|----------|-------------|
| Agent Recruitment and Training Services | Split on commissions | Real estate agents, team leaders, broker owners nationwide | Recruit agents into brokerage, provide training, systems, ISAs, daily sales training calls, listing presentation calls, masterclasses and scripts |

**Agent Recruitment Process:**

**Ideal Agent Profile:**
- Top producers
- Team leaders  
- Broker owners
- Agents looking to build teams under them

**Lead Generation for Recruitment:**
- Reverse prospecting: Listings and agents setting appointments with other agents
- Research their team structure during calls
- Target other high-producing agents
- Call team leaders and broker owners during conversations

**Recruitment Call Framework:**
- Identify their pain points/struggles
- Offer solutions to their challenges
- Book follow-up call with Michelle and/or Cooley
- Michelle/Cooley deepen relationship: Share vision of what they could build/scale to

**Follow-Up Process:**
- Add to social media for ongoing visibility
- DM their stories to stay engaged
- Weekly calls for follow-up
- Annual events to invite them to
- Send weekly agent newsletter (ideal)

**Recruitment Timeline:**
- Slow, relationship-based follow-up until ready to recruit/switch

**Value Proposition - What We Offer Agents & Team Leaders:**

**Daily Sales Training (9:30-10:00 AM CT):**
- Intense sales training led by Chad Cooley (competes with Grant Cardone level)
- Features top producers like Andy Elliott
- Training on seller/buyer conversion, sales psychology

**Daily Support Calls (10:30-11:00 AM CT):**
- Personal coaching from Chad Cooley
- Accountability to goals
- Problem-solving for appointments and sales calls
- Personalized DM outreach and calls

**Weekly Classes:**
- **Wednesdays:** Listing presentation training - Converting in the living room
- **Tuesdays:** Masterclasses with top producers sharing tactical strategies

**Team Building Support (for Team Leaders):**
- Personalized recruiter to build your team
- Chad Cooley coaches based on your vision
- Focus on developing producing agents

**Passive Income Building (for Individual Agents):**
- Individual agent recruiter for downline building
- Revenue share from recruited agents

**Marketing & Design Support:**
- Dedicated graphics design team
- Marketing and CRM specialists
- Custom marketing materials for personal branding and listings

**Technology & Systems:**
- Bold Trail CRM (free)
- Data polling lists for expireds, FSBOs, circle prospecting
- Michelle Wilson provides backend systems setup

**Marketing Services:**
- Top-tier lead generation system
- Automated systems, funnels, and follow-ups (pre-built)
- Branded marketing under agent's name
- Inbound lead funnels with dedicated agent assignment

**Scaling Support:**
- VA vetting and training for cold calling and backend systems
- Support for any scaling needs

**Current state:** So far, my business is at least 16 agents under me and scaling to recruit 2 a month and wanting to get to 50 agents by the end of the year
