**Platform:** YouTube (long-form) + Instagram Reel (cut down) + Story
**Format:** Talking head to camera — this is a story, tell it like one
**Audience:** Sellers — this is the highest-converting content type you have

**YouTube Title:** *"This Home Sat on the Market for 2 Years. Here's How We Finally Sold It."*

**Script structure (talking head — film in front of a sold sign or at the property):**

*[Hook — 0–10 sec]*
> "This home had been sitting on the market for two years. Two years. The seller had tried everything. Different agents, different prices, different strategies. Nothing worked. Then they called me."

*[Relatable bridge — 10–30 sec]*
> "If you've ever watched a home sit and not sell, you know how demoralizing that is. For a seller, every week that goes by is money out of their pocket and stress they didn't sign up for. This family had basically given up. They were about to take it off the market entirely."

*[What you did differently — 30–90 sec]*
> "Here's what we changed: [explain your actual strategy — pricing, marketing, staging, buyer targeting — be specific]. Most agents would have done what the last two did. We didn't. We treated it like a launch, not a listing."

*[Result]*
> "We had it under contract in [X days/weeks]. After two years of nothing."

*[CTA — 3 times: early, mid, end]*
- Early: *"Subscribe — I break down real deals like this every week"*
- Mid: *"If your home has been sitting and you're frustrated — DM me STUCK and I'll tell you exactly what I see"*
- End: *"Link below — find out what your home is actually worth right now. Free, takes 2 minutes."*

**Instagram Reel (cut to 30–45 sec):**
> Hook: *"This home sat on the market for 2 years before they called me."*
> Result: *"We sold it in [X days]."*
> CTA: *"DM me STUCK if your home isn't moving."*

**Story:** Before/after graphic — "2 years unsold → sold in [X days]" — simple, visual, shocking
