## YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION

My coach didn't sugarcoat it.

He told me I was a transaction donkey — just doing donkey work to get paid, not demanding excellence from myself, and then expecting him to do it for me.

It stung. And it was exactly what I needed to hear.

This is a 90-second clip from a real coaching session. No edits. No filter. Just the mirror moment I needed.

**The lesson:**
- You can't expect accountability from someone else if you're not holding yourself accountable first
- There are agents who build something — and agents who just grind transactions
- That's a choice. And it's yours.

If this hit different, you're probably not being a donkey. You're just tired of it.
