## OUTLINE

**Step 1 — Build the Buyer Avatar**
• Every home has a most likely buyer — and it's not "anyone who can afford it"
• For Sunterra specifically: who is moving into this neighborhood? (demographics, lifestyle, stage of life)
• Example avatars to identify for this listing:
  — Young families relocating from Houston proper for schools + space
  — Move-up buyers upgrading from a starter home
  — Corporate relocation buyers on a timeline
• Why knowing this changes everything about how you market the home
[B-roll: you writing out buyer profiles on a whiteboard or notepad]
[B-roll: neighborhood — pool, park, community amenities that appeal to families]

**Step 2 — Create Content for Each Avatar**
• One listing, multiple videos — each one speaks to a different buyer
• Avatar 1 (young family): video showing schools, community pool, backyard space, safety
• Avatar 2 (move-up buyer): video focused on the upgrade — size, finishes, neighborhood feel vs. where they are now
• Avatar 3 (relocation buyer): neighborhood lifestyle video — what it's like to live in Sunterra, commute, community
• Each video is short (60–90 sec), shot on location, posted as organic content AND run as a targeted ad
[B-roll: you filming on location — backyard, community pool, street/park if accessible]
[B-roll: phone showing a short video clip being posted to Instagram/Facebook]

**Step 3 — Targeted Ad Distribution**
• Organic reach is not enough — you have to put the content in front of the right people
• Facebook/Instagram ad targeting for this listing:
  — Audience 1: Houston zip codes, households with children, HHI $120K+
  — Audience 2: Current homeowners in Katy/Energy Corridor who are likely move-up buyers
  — Audience 3: People who recently searched "moving to Houston" or engaged with relocation content
• Budget: $10–15/day per avatar during the listing's first 2 weeks on market
[B-roll: Facebook Ads Manager screen showing audience targeting setup (blur personal info)]
[B-roll: ad preview on phone — video showing the listing]

**Step 4 — Retargeting**
• Anyone who watches 50%+ of the video gets added to a retargeting audience
• They see the listing again — a second touch with a different angle (price, open house, CTA)
• This is how you turn a passive viewer into an active buyer inquiry
• Most agents get one shot in front of a buyer — this gets them 3 or 4
[B-roll: you at your laptop reviewing ad performance / video views / engagement metrics]

**The Result**
• The right buyer finds this home because I put it directly in front of them
• Not waiting for them to stumble on it in an MLS search
• Every listing I take gets this — The Avatar Listing Launch
