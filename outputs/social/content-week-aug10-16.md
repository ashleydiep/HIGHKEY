# Social Media Week — August 10–16, 2026

**This week's themes:**
- Leadership positioning — who you are, how you think, what you've built
- Closings as proof — Affirmed Way + Coppersmith

### MONDAY, Aug 10 — Leadership: The Standard You Hold

**Platform:** Instagram Reel + LinkedIn
**Format:** Talking head, 60–90 sec — direct to camera, no fluff
**Audience:** Agents primarily, sellers secondarily

**Concept:** *"The standard I hold myself to that most agents don't talk about"*

This is a leadership positioning piece. You're not talking about deals — you're talking about the decisions you make every day that most agents avoid.

**Script (talking head):**
> "Here's something I don't hear agents talk about enough — the standard you hold yourself to when nobody's watching.
>
> Not when your broker is looking. Not when a client is on the phone. When it's 7:30am on a Monday and you have to decide: do I dial, or do I find a reason not to?
>
> I've built a business by making the harder call — literally and figuratively — more often than I skip it.
>
> That's not a talent. It's a decision. Every single morning.
>
> The agents I want around me aren't the ones with the best pitch. They're the ones who make that decision consistently.
>
> If that's you — I want to talk."

**Caption:**
> The standard you hold yourself to when nobody's watching — that's your actual brand.
>
> Not your headshots. Not your Zillow reviews. The decision you make at 7:30am when it would be easy to do nothing.
>
> That's what I'm building around. That's the culture.
>
> DM me "COLLAB" if this is how you think.

**Stories (3 slides after posting):**
- Slide 1: *"Most agents talk about results. I talk about the standard that creates them."*
- Slide 2: Poll — *"Do you have a non-negotiable morning routine?"* Yes, locked in / Still working on it
- Slide 3: *"Anyone who voted 'Still working on it' — DM me. Let's fix that."*
