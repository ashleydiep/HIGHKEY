## 7. The Entrepreneur View — Zooming Out

You're not just an agent making calls. You're running three parallel revenue streams. Here's how to see the business clearly:

### Revenue Engine 1: PROAGENT (Listings & Closings)
**What it looks like from the outside:** You make calls, set appts, take listings, close deals.
**What it actually is:** A lead generation and conversion machine. Every contact is a top-of-funnel input. Your KPIs are: contacts → appts → listings → closings. Right now your listing conversion is strong (3 in one week). The gap is *contacts → appts attended*. 13 set MTD — how many have actually been sat?

**The lever to pull:** Appt attendance rate. If you're setting appts but not sitting them, the system leaks. Tighten follow-up between set and met.

### Revenue Engine 2: COLLAB (Agent Recruitment)
**What it looks like from the outside:** You call agents, run discoveries, do 3-ways.
**What it actually is:** A B2B sales pipeline. Each agent recruited = recurring monthly revenue ($500–$2,000/month). 16 agents × average $750/month = ~$12,000/month in recurring revenue at current size. At 50 agents, that's $37,500+/month without closing a single deal yourself.

**The lever to pull:** Speed through the funnel. Discovery → 3-way → verbal → onboard should happen in under 14 days. Where are agents stalling? Map it in GHL.

### Revenue Engine 3: HIGH KEY SOLUTIONS (AI Systems)
**What it looks like:** Pre-revenue, building.
**What it actually is:** The highest-leverage business you're building. One productized AI system sold to 10 agents = more revenue than 10 closings, with zero showing time. Every system you build for yourself is a product you can sell.

**The lever to pull:** Document everything you build. The AI systems running your business right now are the product. When are you taking your first external client?

### How to Think About Time Allocation
| Business | Time Required Now | Revenue Now | Revenue Potential |
|---------|------------------|------------|-----------------|
| PROAGENT | High (active prospecting) | Primary | Capped by hours |
| COLLAB | Medium (pipeline calls) | Growing | Scales with agents |
| High Key Solutions | Low (building in background) | $0 | Uncapped |

**The goal:** As COLLAB grows and HKS generates revenue, reduce PROAGENT time gradually. You're building toward a business that doesn't require you on the phones 8 hours a day.

### The Question to Ask Every Week
*"Am I working in the business or on the business today?"*
- Working **in** it: making calls, running appts, closing deals (necessary now)
- Working **on** it: building systems, recruiting agents, creating content that generates inbound (builds the machine)

Both matter. The ratio should be shifting toward **on** over time.
