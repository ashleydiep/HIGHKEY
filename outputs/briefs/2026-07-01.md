### Expired Call Script

**Opening:**
> "Hi, is this [Name]? Hey, my name is Ashley Diep — I'm a real estate agent here in [area]. I noticed your home on [address] came off the market yesterday and I wanted to reach out. I know that's probably not how you hoped June would end — do you have 2 minutes?"

**If they stay on:**
> "I'm not going to waste your time with the same pitch you've heard. I'll just tell you what I do differently: my listings average 2 weeks to sell. The Houston average right now is 4 months. That gap is the system — pricing strategy, the way I market it, the buyers I reach. If you're open to it, I'd love to pull your numbers and show you exactly what went wrong and what I'd do differently. No obligation."

**If they ask what went wrong with their listing:**
> "Honestly, without seeing everything — it's usually one of three things: price, presentation, or reach. I can give you a real answer in about 10 minutes on the phone. Want to do that now, or is later today better?"

**CTA — book the appointment:**
> "I can come by and do a quick walkthrough and market analysis — I'll show you exactly what comparable homes sold for this month and what your home is realistically worth right now. I have [time] or [time] available today. Which works?"

**If they say no:**
> "Totally understand. Can I ask — are you planning to re-list, take it off the market, or still figuring it out? Even if it's not with me, I'm happy to answer any questions about the market. No pressure either way."

| Objection | Response |
