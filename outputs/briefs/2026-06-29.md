## 4. Circle Prospecting — How to Run It Today

### What to Pull in Mojo Before You Start (do this at 9:30am)
- Pull neighbors within 500ft of each of your 4 active home listings (50–100 numbers each)
- Pull neighbors within 500ft of any listings that went under contract in the last 30 days (your 2 + nearby comps)
- Prioritize: absentee owners + equity-rich homeowners (more likely to sell)

### Call Script

> "Hey [Name], this is Ashley Diep — I'm a real estate agent here in [neighborhood]. I just listed / just sold a home on [street name] and wanted to make sure your neighbors heard about it before it hits everywhere else. Have you had any thoughts about the value of your home lately?"

**Pain-point bridge if they bite:**
> "A lot of homeowners in [area] are surprised when I pull comps for them — values have shifted. Would it be worth 10 minutes for me to just run your numbers? No obligation, purely so you know where you stand."

**If they say no:**
> "Totally fine. Can I ask — do you know anyone in the neighborhood who has thought about making a move? Sometimes the best referrals come from neighbors."

### Tagging in Mojo After Each Call
- Appointment set → tag + immediately move to GHL
- Follow-up (interested, not ready) → HOT or WARM tag + set callback date
- Not interested → tag and move on — don't burn more than 60 seconds
- No answer → drop into auto-dial sequence
