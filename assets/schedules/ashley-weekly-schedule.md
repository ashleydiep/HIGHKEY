## Content Plan

### What to Post Each Day
| Day | Content Type | Format |
|-----|-------------|--------|
| Monday | Market insight or tip | Reel or carousel |
| Wednesday | Closing story or testimonial | Reel or graphic |
| Friday | Personal — behind the scenes, mindset, your story | Story or short video |
| Saturday | Listing or neighborhood content | Reel or post |

### Brainstorm Angles (Rotate Weekly)
- What I told a seller this week that changed their mind
- Behind the scenes of a listing appointment
- The #1 mistake agents make in their first year
- What builders don't tell you when buying new construction
- How I find listings before they hit the market
- A day in the life with an ISA
- Market update — what sold, what didn't, and why
- A phone call moment that stood out this week (great for raw, authentic content)

### Saturday Recording Best Practices
- Film 4–6 pieces in one session — batch everything
- Keep raw clips under 60 seconds, edit down from there
- No fancy setup needed: ring light, phone, clean background
- Capture B-roll while working — at listings, on the phone, driving
- Monday brainstorm feeds Saturday — ideas are fresh from the week

## Call Review System

### Daily (15–20 min — after Co-Lab Call)
- Pull 1 call from your block + 1 from Josh's block
- Note: what worked, what to fix, what to drill in tomorrow's roleplay
- Update Josh's coaching log (2 min)

### Weekly Deep Review (Wednesday — 30 min)
- Pick 3 calls total: 2 of Josh's, 1 of yours
- Score each on the call scorecard: opening, tone, objection handling, close attempt
- Identify Josh's #1 weak spot → becomes Friday roleplay focus
- Share feedback before the Friday 1:1 so Josh can self-reflect first

---

## ISA 1:1 Friday Agenda (30 min)

1. **Numbers** (5 min) — dials, contacts, appts set, appts sat for the week
2. **Call recordings** (15 min) — listen to 2 calls together, pause and coach
3. **Skill drill** (5 min) — drill the one objection or close technique to work on
4. **One fix** (3 min) — one specific thing Josh commits to improving by next Friday
5. **Win** (2 min) — call out something he did well this week
