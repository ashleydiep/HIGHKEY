# Follow-Up Sequences: Cancelled Appointments + Post-Meeting No-Lists

**Part of:** 180-Day Follow-Up System (Asset Build — due Aug 31, 2026)
**Load into:** GHL automation
**Tone across both sequences:** Warm, no pressure, value-first — never chasing, always relevant

---

## SEQUENCE 1: CANCELLED APPOINTMENT

**Who this is for:** Sellers who set an appointment and no-showed, cancelled, or went quiet before the meeting.

**Goal:** Re-engage, reset the appointment, stay relevant until they're ready. Most of these people WILL sell — they just weren't ready yet. The agent who stays in front of them wins.

**Trigger:** Appointment tagged as cancelled or no-show in GHL/Mojo.

---

### DAY 0 — Same Day (Text)

Send within 2 hours of missed appointment.

> "Hey [Name] — Ashley here. Looks like we missed each other today — no worries at all, life gets busy. I had some solid numbers pulled for your area that I think you'd find interesting. What does your schedule look like this week to connect?"

*If no response by end of day:*

> "No pressure at all [Name] — just want to make sure you have my info when the timing is right. Ashley Diep, [number]. Happy to connect whenever works for you."

## SEQUENCE 2: POST-APPOINTMENT — DECIDED NOT TO LIST

**Who this is for:** Sellers who met with Ashley (the appointment happened), heard the pitch, but decided not to list — either went with another agent, decided to wait, or said they need to think about it.

**Goal:** Stay top of mind without pressure. Be the second call when their agent fails them, or when they finally decide to move. This is a long game — handled right, these become listings 3–6 months later.

**Tone:** Grateful, generous, zero bitterness. You're the professional they'll remember.

**Trigger:** Contact tagged as "Met — Did Not List" in GHL.

---

### DAY 0 — Same Day (Text — Send Within 2 Hours)

> "Hey [Name] — Ashley here. Really enjoyed meeting with you today. I know you've got a big decision ahead — no pressure from my end. If you have any questions as you're thinking it through, I'm always a call away. Good luck with whatever you decide."

## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

**Load into GHL:**
- Create two separate workflows: "Cancelled Appt" and "Met — Did Not List"
- Tag contacts at point of trigger (Mojo or GHL)
- All texts send from your GHL number
- Emails send from your personal email or GHL email
- Pause sequence if contact books a new appointment or lists with you

**Personalization fields to populate when tagging:**
- `{first_name}`
- `{their_area}` — neighborhood or zip
- `{price_range}` — rough home value estimate from the appointment
- `{their_situation}` — one-line note from the appointment (vacant, upsizing, divorce, etc.)
- `{agent_note}` — what stood out from the conversation (use in Day 1 emails)

**Kill switch:**
- If they list with you → remove from sequence immediately
- If they ask to be removed → remove immediately, tag as "Do Not Contact"
- If they sell with another agent → move to "Past Seller — Referral Nurture" sequence (build next)
