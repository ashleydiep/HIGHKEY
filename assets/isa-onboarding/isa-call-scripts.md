# ISA Call Scripts
## PROAGENT — Cold Calling Script Reference

> Use these scripts as guides. Customize the company name and agent name for every call.
> **Always repeat and affirm the prospect's responses.**

---

## Initial Database / Sphere of Influence Script

> Use when calling people you already know — past clients, referrals, database contacts.

"Hey ______, this is [your name], how are you?"

*(Insert any personal small talk)*

"I don't want to take up too much of your time, I'm sure you're busy right now. I made a commitment to reach out to all of the people that I know because there have been major changes taking place in the real estate market. I wanted to call you directly to see if you've seen what's been going on?

With all of the movement happening right now, I just expanded into the real estate business with one of the top teams in the country, so that I can better service my clients and provide the best resources and service possible.

______ have you thought about making any moves or changes now or in the future?"

*(If No)*

"Ok great! I just want you to know that I am a resource for you or anyone that you know whether it's buying or selling, or any services or resources that you need for your property. I can also keep you up to date on all of the major changes that are taking place monthly... what's the best email address for you?"

> Use this opportunity to fill in any missing information — email, address, etc.

## Opening Scripts by Lead Type

### Circle Prospecting — Just Listed

"Hi [prospect's first name]! My name is _____ with [company]. I am calling because we listed a property for sale over there in [street name]. Would you know anyone who might be interested in this neighborhood?"

**If YES →** "Fantastic! What is your email address? I can send you the detailed report of the property we listed and also for you to forward it to the people you know that are interested in moving in this neighborhood."
- Then: "What about you, do you have any plans on moving in the near future?"
  - YES → "Great! I can have our listing agent join this call so they could discuss all necessary information and confirm a time to meet."
  - NO → "I see! The reason why I am asking you is because your property is located in the hottest market in real estate now. So, if you are thinking of moving or selling your house this is the best time for you to do that. Is there any dollar amount that can make you think of moving or selling your house?"

**If NO →** "I see! I truly appreciate you taking time to try and help."
- Then: "What about you, do you have any plans on moving in the near future?" *(same flow as YES above)*

**If I'm Interested →** *(Buyer qualification — see buyer questions below)*

---

### Circle Prospecting — Just Sold

"Hi [prospect's first name]! My name is _____ with [company]. I am calling because we just sold a property over there in [street name]. Do you know anyone interested in buying or selling real estate?"

**If Selling →** Proceed with motivation questions and appointment close.

**If Buying →** *(See buyer qualification questions below)*

### Circle Prospecting — Pending Sale

"Hi [prospect's first name]! My name is _____ with [company]. I am calling because our listed property for sale over there in [street name] already got an offer. Do you know anyone interested in buying or selling real estate?"

*(Same flow as Just Sold)*

### Expired Listings

"Good morning/afternoon. May I speak with [prospect name]?"

*(Once connected)*

"Hi [prospect's first name]! I'm _____ from [company]. I'm calling about the property over there at [address]. I would like to know if it's still for sale."

**If YES →**
"That's great! We saw your property came OFF the market and we were wondering what's stopping the property from getting sold.
- Have you got an offer while it was on the market?
- Did you have any open houses?
- Did you get any feedback from your agent why your property did not sell?"

→ Proceed to motivation questions → appointment close.

---

### FSBO (For Sale By Owner)

"Good morning/afternoon. May I speak with [prospect name]?"

*(Once connected)*

"Hi [prospect's first name]! I'm _____ from [company]. I'm calling about the property over there at [address]. I would like to know if it's still for sale."

**If YES →**
"That's great! We saw your property came ON the market. Have you received any attention on the property?
- Do you live in the property?
- Why did you decide to sell your property by yourself rather than getting a real estate agent?
- I'm surprised that a home like yours has not sold!"

→ Proceed to motivation questions → appointment close.

### Land

"Hi! I'm _____ with [company]. I am calling about your property at [address]. Is it still for sale?"

**If YES →**
"Great, I am calling you to let you know that we can help you sell that vacant land. We have a database full of builders and people wanting to build homes and we have agents who specialize in connecting builders with vacant land and are experienced negotiating with them.

Let me ask:
- How much are you going to sell the land for?
- How big is it?"

→ Proceed to appointment close.

## Not the Prospect / Gatekeeper

"Would you be authorized to speak on the owner's behalf?"
- **YES →** Proceed as normal
- **NO →** "May I know what's the best time to call back to reach out to the owner?" *(note callback time)* "Thank you so much and have a great day."

## Property Already Sold / Listed / Under Contract

**SOLD:**
"That is great news! In the future, in case you have any plans of selling your property, or you know someone who does, feel free to call us back at [number] to discuss how we can get the best deal out of your property. We would love to help you sell your property at top dollar. This is _____ of [company]. Thank you so much and have a great day!"

**UNDER CONTRACT:**
"That is great news! We hope that you got the top dollar amount for your property. In the future, in case you have any plans of selling your property, or you know someone who does, feel free to call us back at [number]. This is _____ of [company]. Thank you so much and have a great day!"

**ALREADY LISTED:**
"That is great news! We hope that you sell the house at top dollar in the shortest time possible! Now in case your house does not sell, feel free to call us back at [number] to discuss how we can net you top dollar within the shortest reasonable amount of time. This is _____ of [company]. Thank you so much and have a great day!"

## Motivation Questions

> Once you have a live conversation, dig into motivation. Always wait for the answer.

- "If you sold this home, where would you go next?"
- "How soon do you need to get there?"
- "What takes you down there?"
- "What's the most important thing to you in getting this home sold?"
- "What's your number one goal in selling your home/property?"
- "Are you going to buy a new house or just want to cash out?"
- "Where are you going to buy a new house?"
- "Are you downsizing or upsizing?"

> **Always repeat and affirm the prospect's responses.**

---

## Buyer Qualification Questions

> Use when a prospect says they are interested in buying.

- "May I know in which city you are looking to buy a home?"
- "How many bedrooms and bathrooms are you looking for?"
- "Are you going to buy the house in cash or financing?"
- "If financing, which bank have you acquired pre-approval from?"
- "If financing but without pre-approval, which bank are you going to get pre-approval from?"
- "How soon would you want to move?"

→ If qualified buyer: "I am going to get this property's listing agent on the line so we can talk about how you can purchase this property." *(Live transfer to agent)*

## Follow-Up Call — After Agent Appointment

> Use the day after the prospect met with the agent.

"Hi, this is _____. I work with [agent name] and I understand that you met with them yesterday. I wanted to call and find out how everything went."

*(WAIT FOR THE ANSWER)*

**Everything Went Great →**
"Great! LET'S DO THIS! I am going to get [agent name] on the line and get everything set up. Please hold."

**Interviewing Other Agents →**
"So you are planning on interviewing more agents. What did you like best about my agent? *(wait)* Great — how many agents are you scheduled to meet with at this point? *(wait)* What's the most important thing that you are looking for in an agent that winds up listing your home? *(wait)* Was there anything that you thought my agent could do to earn your business? *(wait)*

The best thing about this program is it's really aggressive — the first thing we do when we get your listing active is reach out to all the agents in the area, including the ones you're interviewing with, to make sure they're bringing their buyers as well. Did [agent name] mention that to you in the appointment?

Okay great — let me get [agent name] on the line so they can explain better. Please stay on the line."

**Wants to Hold Off →**
"Do you know what's great about what you just said? Everybody is thinking the same exact thing — they want to hold off and sell in [time frame]. Do you know what that means for you? It means less competition — and if you want to demand a higher asking price for your property, the best time to do it is now. So let's do this — let me get [agent name] on the line and see if they can make it make sense for you right now."

**Didn't Agree on Price or Commission →**
"Okay. So you have a certain number you need to hit in order for this to make sense for you financially, right? *(wait)* But the last thing you want to do is have your property sit on the market for another couple of months and wind up in the same position — is that fair to say? *(wait)* Okay, before we make any decisions — LET'S DO THIS. I'm going to get [agent name] back on the line to see if they can make this situation make sense for you financially. Please hold."

## Common Objections & Responses

### "Do you have a buyer?"
"That's actually why we are calling! We have a database of buyers who are actively looking for properties in your neighborhood."

### "I can sell on my own / go FSBO"
"I totally understand the thought of trying to get a home sold yourself. Let's face it — saving that commission can mean some good money in your pocket, right? Commission aside, if we can net you more money than you can net on your own, then obviously it would make sense for you, right?"

### "Why would I pay a commission?"
"Great question. We are working with tons of buyers and sellers out there and that's exactly what we do. We specialize in generating multiple offers for our listings because what we do is we go out and we actively pursue buyers."

### "What assurance do I have that you can sell my house?"
"If you think we are not confident to get your property sold, at least get a second opinion from one of the top teams in the country. I do not want you to do anything unless it makes sense for you financially."

### "Not interested"
"If you were to get an offer on this property at this point, would you still be willing to take it?"

### "I've heard the same before / Not good experience with previous agent"
"I understand your frustration. I hear this every day. Most agents talk a big game to get your listing and end up sticking a sign in the yard and listing it online just waiting for someone to stop by. You can do that yourself — no need to pay a commission for that, right? The reason you want an agent isn't because you don't want to work with an agent — it's that you want an agent that's going to work for you, right?"

### "I already have an agent"
"Have you signed a contract with the agent?"
- YES → "Thank you so much for your time and have a great day!"
- NO → "I understand you already picked a new agent but it's always nice to have a second opinion. I don't want you to sign another multi-dollar contract if our presentation won't be enough. You can always stop it and we can go our separate ways. The appointment is not an obligation to sign a contract."

### "Need to talk to my husband/wife first"
"I understand that you and your husband/wife are both on the same page when you decide to put your property on the market — is that right? I'm going to ask our top agent to overnight you our pre-listing package. It's going to go over some things about our company and the resources we use to market your home. I'd like for you both to take a look at it, write down all your questions, and by the time our top agent is there that's the first thing you'll be discussing."

### "Send me an email"
"I'm happy to send you an email but what would an email do to sell your property? What I'm going to do is one more step forward — I'm going to ask our top agent to overnight you our pre-listing package. It's going to go over some things about our company, our top agent, and the resources we use to market your home. I would like for you to take a look at it and have enough time to write down all your questions. So by the time our top agent arrives, that's the first thing you'll be discussing."

### "Will wait for a couple of months"
"Well, it sounds to me that you still want to get your property sold — you just want to do it in a couple of months. Are you prepared to take less money in a couple of months when you can get top dollar now? Because other homeowners are pulling their home off the market and will wait too. Do you know what that means for you? LESS COMPETITION IN A HOT MARKET. That way, we can demand a higher asking price NOW than you could in a couple of months."

### "I'm going to sell my property soon"
"Well it sounds to me that you still want to get your property sold — you just want to do it in [time frame]. If you get this property sold today, where would you go next? *(wait)* That's exciting! What takes you down there? *(wait)* Great — how soon do you have to be there? *(wait)*"

### "I will list with a friend"
"Have you signed a contract?"
- NO → "I understand you want to list with a friend but it's always nice to have a second opinion. Now let me ask you this — what do you think your agent will do differently to actually get your home sold? I'm sure your agent did the best job they can do with the resources they have, but at the end of the day it all comes down to the resources used, right?"

### "Property is rented"
"That's great! How long is the lease agreement for? I understand you decided to rent the place which is your Plan B, right? Are you aware that having a lease in place and renters in it makes your property more valuable? Would you rather collect a monthly rental fee OR get the paycheck for your property? If I can show you a way to have your property sold to investors for TOP DOLLAR without pulling out the renters — then definitely it would make sense to you, right?"

### "Rescue Question" (last resort before hanging up)
"If you were to get an offer on this property at this point, would you still be willing to take it?"
"I believe you already established the idea of selling — it's not a matter of if but a matter of when. Are you willing to take less money than you can get right now?"
