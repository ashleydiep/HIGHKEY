## Call Block Rules

1. **No distractions during call blocks** — do not disturb, no social media, no side conversations
2. **Disposition every call before moving to the next** — never leave a call untagged in MOJO
3. **Import to GHL immediately** when an appointment is set — do not wait until end of day
4. **Leave a voicemail on every unanswered call** — no silent hang-ups
5. **Update GHL notes after every live conversation** — date, what was said, next step

---

## Appointment Standards

An appointment is not set until all three steps are complete:

1. **Date and time confirmed** with the seller on the call
2. **Calendar invite created** and Ashley invited
3. **Contact imported to GHL** with full details — name, address, phone, notes

If any of these three steps are missing, the appointment does not count.

---

## Reporting Requirements

### Daily (every working day)
- Fill out the Daily Rolling Report via email by end of session
  - Start time, end time
  - Dials, contacts, appointments set, appointments gone on, listings taken
  - List every appointment with: name | date set | address | platform ID
  - Additional notes on hot leads or callbacks

### Weekly
- ISA Tracker (Google Sheet) updated and current
- Share sheet with Ashley, Cooley, and Michelle

---

## GHL Standards

Every contact you speak with must have:
- **Lead category tag** (Expireds, FSBO, Absentee, Vacant, Circle Prospecting, etc.)
- **ISA tag** — on every contact you touch
- **Notes** — date of call, what was discussed, next step

If it is not in GHL, it did not happen.

---

## Performance Expectations

### Green (On Track)
- 100+ dials/day
- 1+ appointment/day
- Report submitted daily
- GHL fully updated

### Yellow (Needs Improvement)
- 75–99 dials/day
- Less than 1 appointment/day average over the week
- Reports submitted inconsistently

### Red (Action Required)
- Below 75 dials/day consistently
- No appointments in a week
- Missing reports or GHL updates

> Performance is reviewed weekly. Two yellow weeks = coaching call. Red week = immediate review.

---

## What Success Looks Like at 90 Days

- 150+ dials per day
- 2–3 appointments set per day
- 10–15 appointments per week feeding the listing pipeline
- Fully self-sufficient on list pulling, MOJO setup, and GHL management
